## Alarm of a single station/Sigen EV AC Charger

1. On the "Home" screen, click the station name you want to query.
2. Click <img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/0 (1).png" alt="" data-size="line"> next to the station name and click "Notice" to view the alarm of this station.
