# Routine Maintenance

To ensure the long-term operation of the equipment, you are advised to perform routine maintenance according to this section.

If so, clean it up. Do not use tools that may cause electric shock or insulation damage, such as wire brushes during the cleaning process.</td><td>Yes</td><td>Once every three months.</td></tr><tr><td>System operating state</td><td><ul><li>Check whether the equipment is damaged or deformed.</li><li>Listen for any abnormal noises during the operation of the equipment.</li><li>When the equipment is operating, check whether the equipment parameters are correctly set.</li></ul></td><td>No</td><td>Once every six months.</td></tr></tbody></table>
