# Stop Charging

### **Charging completed**

The equipment will automatically stop charging when the vehicle is fully charged.

### **During charging**

* **Method 1: During charging**

Read your Sigen RFID Card to stop charging.

* **Method 2: App authenticated**

Stop charging from "Home"→![](../../../.gitbook/assets/DC.png)→"Stop".

{% include "../../../.gitbook/includes/waring.md" %}

* <mark style="color:orange;">**If the charging connector is locked because SigenStor EVDC 12 (7.5GBT, 10GBT) or SigenStor EVDC 25 (7.5GBT, 10GBT) stops charging upon fault, you can press the button shown in the figure to unlock the charging connector.**</mark>

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/1 (7).png>)

* <mark style="color:orange;">**When the equipment is charging normally or the charging connector has metal damage, do not perform the steps shown in the figure.**</mark>
