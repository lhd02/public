# Team and company management

If the installer company account number wants to grant permissions to other installers to view and set up your power station, or if you want to view and set up the power stations of other installers, click on "Setting" → "Company Management" to configure the settings.

**Authorize other installers:** Join the team with invitation code. You can join only one team.

**View other installers:** Copy "My Invitation Code" to the invitee and invite him to join your team.
