# Others

## Changing account password

**Method 1:**

On the login screen, click "Forgot Password" to reset the login password.

**Method 2:**

Click "Setting" and ![](<.gitbook/assets/image (2).png>) on the screen top to change "Password."

## Changing account nickname

Click "Setting" and ![](<.gitbook/assets/image (3).png>) on the screen top to modify "Nickname.”

## Changing account binding information

Click "Setting" and ![](<.gitbook/assets/image (1).png>) on the screen top to change "Binding Information," for example, email address.

## Viewing and exchanging points

Click "Setting" → "My Points" to view point details. You can also redeem your points for rewards.

## Team and company management

If the installer company account number wants to grant permissions to other installers to view and set up your power station, or if you want to view and set up the power stations of other installers, click on "Setting" → "Company Management" to configure the settings.

**Authorize other installers**: Join the team with invitation code. You can join only one team.

**View other installers**: Copy "My Invitation Code" to the invitee and invite him to join your team.

## Viewing App version

Click "Setting" → "About" to go to the viewing screen.

## Owner consultation and request management

Click "Service" → "Service CRM" or "Dispatched" to check and manage owner consultation requests.
