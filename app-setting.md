# App Setting

## Viewing App version

Click "Setting" → "About" to go to the viewing screen.

## Upgrading mySigen

{% include ".gitbook/includes/tips.md" %}

<mark style="color:blue;">To gain the best compatibility and performance, you are advised to upgrade the mySigen App regularly.</mark>

Click "Setting" → "About" → "Version Update" and execute the upgrade process.
