# Residual Current Protection Standards and Recommendations for Inverters

&#x20;The RCMU can operate normally in the HYB inverter under both grid-connected and off-grid conditions.

When the residual current exceeds the preset threshold (see below table), the inverter will disconnect within the time limit to protect against possible electric shock and fire hazard. This value is set according to section 4.8.3.5 in IEC62109-2 as below table.&#x20;

<table><thead><tr><th valign="top">Inverter Capacity</th><th valign="top">Max. Continuous Residual Current Limit</th><th valign="top">Time  to  Disconnect  from  the Grid</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td valign="top">Rated Continuous Power ＞ 30kVA</td><td valign="top">10mA per KVA</td><td valign="top">0.3s</td></tr></tbody></table>

In case of a sudden change in residual current, the inverters will disconnect from the grid within the following time specified in the table.

<table><thead><tr><th width="346.22216796875" valign="top">Residual Current Sudden Change</th><th valign="top">Max. Time to Disconnect the Inverter from the Grid</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td valign="top">30mA</td><td valign="top">0.3s</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">60mA</td><td valign="top">0.15s</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">150mA</td><td valign="top">0.04s</td></tr></tbody></table>
