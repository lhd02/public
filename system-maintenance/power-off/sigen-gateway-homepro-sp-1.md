<mark style="color:orange;">The Gateway should be disconnected in the following order:</mark>

1. <mark style="color:orange;">Turn off the miniature circuit breaker QF4 (connecting to Backup Household loads).</mark>
2. <mark style="color:orange;">Turn off the miniature circuit breaker QF2 (connecting to Generator/Smart load).</mark>
3.  <mark style="color:orange;">After shutting down the inverter on the phone, turn off the miniature circuit breaker QF3</mark>

    <mark style="color:orange;">(connecting to Inverters).</mark>
4. <mark style="color:orange;">Turn off the miniature circuit breaker QF1 (connecting to Power grid).</mark>
5. <mark style="color:orange;">Switch off the surge protective device switch QF5.</mark>
