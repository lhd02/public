<mark style="color:orange;">The Gateway should be disconnected in the following order:</mark>

1. <mark style="color:orange;">Turn off the miniature circuit breaker QF4 (connecting to Backup Household loads).</mark>
2. <mark style="color:orange;">After shutting down the inverter on the phone, turn off the miniature circuit breaker QF2 or QF3 (connecting to Inverters).</mark>
3. <mark style="color:orange;">Turn off the miniature circuit breaker QF1 (connecting to Power grid).</mark>
