# mySigen App Download and Login

{% include "../.gitbook/includes/tips.md" %}

* <mark style="color:blue;">**This document takes version 3.0.0 as an example to introduce relevant operations. The actual screen display shall prevail.**</mark>
* <mark style="color:blue;">**The screen differs slightly between PV charging and PV storage & charging networking, but the operations are the same. The illustrations here are for reference only. The actual screen display shall prevail.**</mark>

1. Download the app.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/SSA1CM00014-欧洲区.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Provide your email account to the installer for signing up.
3. After signing up your account, the installer will ask you to activate your account.
4. Please check the email sent from the "sigencloud" account in your inbox, set your initial password, and activate your account.
5. Log in to the app.
